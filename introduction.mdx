**Eval Protocol is an open standard for connecting RL environments and evaluators to training systems.** It provides a unified, portable way to build evaluations that run on your laptop and scale to any production backend without rewrites.

Reinforcement learning development is often slowed by the gap between local experimentation and production training. Teams constantly rebuild evaluation logic, struggle with reproducibility, and find it difficult to plug their custom environments into distributed training frameworks.

Eval Protocol solves this by standardizing the most critical component: the evaluation loop. This creates a clear, iterative workflow:

1. **Develop Locally**: Implement your evaluation logic once using a simple, standardized interface. Test and debug on your local machine with full observability.
2. **Connect Anywhere**: Plug your portable evaluator into any compatible training backend—from cloud providers to open-source RL libraries.
3. **Scale Confidently**: Launch distributed training jobs, knowing the reward signal and environment logic are identical to what you validated locally.

This collapses reward shaping, environment interaction, and training into a single, reliable loop.

## Who This Is For

- **Applied Teams** who are shipping complex agents (for code-gen, tool-use, etc.) and need a reliable bridge from prototype to production.
- **Research Engineers** who need a fast, reproducible way to test hypotheses locally before scaling up expensive experiments.
- **MLOps Teams** who are responsible for creating stable, observable, and reusable evaluation infrastructure for their entire organization.

## Getting Started

If you already have an agent or application, you can connect it to the protocol without rewriting your core codebase:

- **[Remote Server](/tutorial/remote-rollout-processor)** - Use the `RemoteRolloutProcessor` to delegate rollout execution to any persistent HTTP service.
- **[GitHub Actions](/tutorial/github-actions-rollout)** - Use the `GithubActionRolloutProcessor` to trigger and manage rollouts within your existing CI/CD workflows.
